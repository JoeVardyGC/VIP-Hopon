To use the official Telecel Cash, AT Money or card logos, drop the files here with these exact names:
  telecel.png  (or telecel.svg)
  at.png       (or at.svg)
  card.png     (or card.svg)
Square images about 192 x 192 work best. The app picks them up automatically; until then it shows a drawn mark in the brand's colours.
Get the files from Hubtel's merchant brand kit or the telcos' press pages, and check the usage terms.
